# Interrogatory & Motion Drafter (Demo)

Drafts plaintiff's answers to Form A uniform interrogatories, answers to the defense's supplemental questions, and routine motion papers from the facts of a matter, entered once. Every answer is checked by the paralegal and approved by the attorney before it is served.

**This is a demonstration.**

## Open the demo

- **Front page and instructions:** https://alyssaagard.github.io/interrogatory-motion-drafter/
- **Example:** https://alyssaagard.github.io/interrogatory-motion-drafter/example/
  Opens with a fill-in-the-blank example already entered, so every screen and the finished answers can be seen right away.
- **Blank:** https://alyssaagard.github.io/interrogatory-motion-drafter/blank/
  Starts with no matters, for entering a matter from the beginning. Takes a few seconds to open the first time.

## How it works

1. **Enter the facts** on the first five screens: police report, medical records, client, and attorney decisions. Anything an answer still needs is marked in red.
2. **Review the Form A answers.** Each is drafted from those screens. Choose Fill in next to a missing item to go straight to the right box.
3. **Sign off.** The paralegal marks each answer as checked; the attorney approves it. A later change flags the answer to be checked again.
4. **Supplemental questions.** Paste the defense's questions. Standard answers are suggested, and questions past the tenth or with subparts are flagged with the matching objection.
5. **Finish and serve.** Deadlines, attachment and HIPAA checklists, and the finished answers as a Word file.

**Motion papers** prepares the notice, certification, proposed order, and deadlines for routine motions. **Client questionnaire** prepares a plain-language questionnaire for the client.
